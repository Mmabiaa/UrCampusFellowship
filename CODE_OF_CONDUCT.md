# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

UrCampusFellowship exists to help students from every denomination find their community — so the space where we build it should reflect that same spirit of welcome. We pledge to make participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of denomination, faith background, age, gender, ethnicity, level of experience, or role on the project.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that helps this project thrive:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what's best for the community of students this platform serves
- Showing empathy and patience toward other contributors, especially those newer to the codebase
- Respecting that this platform serves many denominations equally — no chapter, fellowship, or belief should be treated as more important than another in how the product is built or discussed

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind
- Disparaging any specific denomination, fellowship, or faith tradition
- Using this contributor space to proselytize, debate theology, or promote one denomination over another — this project is neutral infrastructure for all of them
- Publishing others' private information (student data, contact details, credentials) without explicit permission
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a shared community project

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages — and in any other setting when someone is representing the project or its community.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, with respect to the privacy of whoever reports an issue. Maintainers who don't follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face consequences determined by the project's other maintainers.

Maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that don't align with this Code of Conduct.

## A note specific to this project

Because UrCampusFellowship is built to serve students across many different denominations fairly, contributors are asked to hold an extra degree of care here: code, copy, sample data, and design choices should never implicitly favor one denomination over another (for example, in the order things are listed, the language used, or which fellowship appears in example screenshots). If you're unsure whether something reads as neutral, ask before merging.
